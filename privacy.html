Room Raid Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Room Raid is made by Brad Senese ("we"). This policy explains what data the game uses.

What we don't do

  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics, and no sale of data.
  • We don't ask you to create an account.

Camera and 3D scanning

The scanner uses your device's camera and LiDAR Scanner to build a 3D model of the buildings, alleyways and streets around you. This happens on your device. Camera images aren't stored or uploaded as photos or video. Scans stay on your device unless you choose to share them.

Game Center

If you use online multiplayer or leaderboards, Apple's Game Center gives the game your Game Center player ID and display name, and records your wins on the leaderboard. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Multiplayer and Local Wi-Fi

When you send a scan or world to other players, online or over Local Wi-Fi, it goes directly to their devices. Local Wi-Fi uses your local network only to find nearby players and send them game data. We don't receive a copy.

Public posts (optional)

Nothing is posted unless you choose Post publicly and confirm. A posted building is stored in Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) public database with an anonymous iCloud record identifier so other players can download it. You can delete your post at any time with Delete my public post. Copies other players already downloaded stay on their devices. Don't post private places, or anything showing people or personal information.

Exports

USDZ exports and map files you share through the share sheet go wherever you send them. We don't receive a copy.

Children

Room Raid is rated 16+. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can sign out of Game Center, choose not to post, delete your public post, and delete the app to remove local data.

Contact

Brad Senese. Questions or removal requests: open an issue.

We'll post any changes to this policy on this page.
