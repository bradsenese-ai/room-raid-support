Room Raid Support

Room Raid is a free twin-stick shooter where you scan buildings, alleyways and streets and defend them from an alien invasion.

Get help

To report a bug, ask a question or request that a public post be removed, email magnificentindicators@gmail.com or open an issue here. Please include your device model and iOS version. We aim to reply within a few days, and handle content reports within 24 hours.

Common questions

Why is the Scan button greyed out? Scanning needs a LiDAR Scanner (iPhone 12 Pro or later Pro models, or iPad Pro 2020 or later). Everything else in the game works on any device running iOS 16 or later.

How do we all play the same building? When starting Co-op or Versus, pick a scan under 12 MB to send to everyone, swap scans in the Build Lobby, or use Local Wi-Fi to send a whole world of any size to nearby players. See How to Play in the app.

Local Wi-Fi can't find other players. Make sure everyone is on the same Wi-Fi with Bluetooth on, and that Local Network is allowed for Room Raid in Settings > Privacy & Security > Local Network.

How do I delete my public post? Tap Delete my public post in the Build Lobby or on Home.

Scan safely. Only scan public places or places you have permission to scan, never people or private information, and watch for traffic.

FAQ

How do I play Troops vs You? Pick it on Home. The troopers hunt you. You win by downing them all.

How do I reach the caverns? Find the glowing key in the big saucer's room in the first outdoor area, pick it up, then use the underground door. "Up to ground level" returns you.

Does it need LiDAR? Only for scanning buildings, alleyways and streets.

Is it free? Yes. There are no ads or purchases.
